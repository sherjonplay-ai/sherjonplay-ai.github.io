YT history

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

"YT history" is a private production tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel The American Time Vault. This policy explains what the app does with Google and YouTube data.

Who can use the app

Only the channel owner uses the app, with their own Google account. The app is not offered to the public and does not collect information from viewers or from any other person.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses YouTube API Services with these scopes:

  • youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl: to upload the channel's own videos, set their title, description, thumbnail, captions and publication time;
  • youtube.readonly: to read the channel's own videos and basic channel details;
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read the channel's own aggregated statistics (views, watch time, audience country and similar totals).

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish the channel's own videos and to plan future videos.
  • The OAuth access token and the channel statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time on the Google security settings page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that, the stored token stops working and can be deleted.

YouTube and Google policies

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the channel page: youtube.com/@american_time_vault.

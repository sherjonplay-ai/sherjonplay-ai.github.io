The American Time Vault

The American Time Vault is a YouTube channel of long-form documentaries about forgotten America: the rise and fall of American brands and stores, Main Street, industry, and everyday life from the 1950s to the 1990s.

Watch the channel: youtube.com/@american_time_vault

About the YT history app

"YT history" is a private production tool used only by the owner of The American Time Vault. With the owner's permission, it uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload the channel's own finished videos as private and schedule their publication;
  • add titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions to those videos;
  • read the channel's own statistics to plan future videos.

It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
